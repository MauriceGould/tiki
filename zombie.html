The Living Dead Maker

Zombie

A famously powerful tiki cocktail that layers multiple rums with citrus, spice, and tropical fruit. The result is complex, aromatic, and meant to be enjoyed slowly.

Prep Time
8 minutes
Servings
1 drink
Ingredients
9

What You'll Need

Ingredients

  • 1 oz light rum
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz overproof rum
  • 3/4 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 oz grapefruit juice
  • 1/2 oz cinnamon syrup
  • 1/2 oz falernum
  • 1 tsp grenadine
  • 1 dash aromatic bitters
  • Mint sprig, for garnish

Mix It Up

Instructions

  1. Add all liquid ingredients to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake briefly until chilled.
  3. Strain into a tall tiki glass filled with crushed ice.
  4. Top with additional crushed ice and garnish with a large mint sprig.

MO'S MOJO Tip: This is a strong cocktail. Serve one per guest and encourage slow, responsible enjoyment.
